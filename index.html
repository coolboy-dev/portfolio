UPI City

A simulated payment network for testing fraud detectors. Because I inject the fraud myself, I know the right answer for all 123,622 payments and can score any detector honestly.

I tested eight. Four did worse than random guessing, and none of the open source ones beat simply sorting payments by amount. Mine was 15 times better than chance.

A C++ web server

About 1,300 lines of C++ with no dependencies. With one event loop per CPU core it handles around 665,000 requests a second, roughly level with nginx on the same laptop, in under 12 MB of memory.

The jump from one loop to many didn't touch the rest of the code: the workers, the cache and the rate limiter stayed the same.

GST bill checker

A cashback app's users were waiting days for their shopping bills to be approved. This checks a bill read by OCR in microseconds and says approve, review or reject, with the reason.

It catches fake or misread GSTINs, totals that were edited, and the same bill uploaded twice. No AI needed, just arithmetic. The demo on the right is the real code running in your browser.
